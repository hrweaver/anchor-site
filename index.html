Why it works

What makes it stick is another person.

Anchor is not about locking yourself down harder. It takes something you have been meaning to sort out on your own and makes it a thing you and someone you trust do together.

Asking out loud

One small moment of asking another person is enough to change what you do next. No streaks to break, no shame, just a quick question.

Someone you chose

You pick who you partner with, and you can change your mind any time. They are on your side because you put them there.

It gets easier

Reaching for the app you blocked stops being automatic sooner than you would think. The habit you build with someone tends to be the one that lasts.

Who's in it with you

Accountability runs both ways.

Anchor is the same idea at every age, and it works best when nobody is doing it alone. Kids learn their phone habits by watching yours. A simpler phone lands very differently when the grown-ups' phones are anchored too.

How it works

Four steps, then it stays out of your way.

Pair with your person

Share a code with your partner, a parent, or a friend. One code is enough to link you both, and you can be paired with more than one person.

Set it up once

Anchor walks you through a few phone permissions so it can actually block, then you pick the apps that pull you in. They stay blocked everywhere, not just on the home screen.

Ask when you need it

Open something blocked and you can send a request. They get a notification, they approve or deny it, and it unlocks for as long as they said. Not a second longer.

They know it is still on

Your people see that your blocking is set up and holding, plus every request you send. If Anchor gets switched off or goes quiet, they are told. No self-reporting, just partnership and accountability.

Our approach

Built on trust, not control.

Anchor only works if you would happily hand it to someone you trust. That rules a few things out on purpose.

Deleting Anchor always works

Uninstall is never blocked. It stays an emergency exit, always. Your partner simply gets told, which is the entire point.

No blocker is unbreakable

Anyone determined enough can get around any of them, and we would rather say so than pretend otherwise.

Status, not surveillance

Your partner sees that your protection is on and the requests you choose to send. They do not see which apps you use, for how long, or what you browse.
