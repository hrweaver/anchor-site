What it is

The dumb phone idea, without buying a second phone.

The phones built for this cost real money and make you carry two devices or give up the apps you need. Anchor Dumb Phone does the same job on the Android you have: your tools stay, everything else stays installed and stays closed.

Your tools, and only those

Phone, Messages, Maps, Camera, Music and the rest of the set a dumb phone ships with, plus up to three apps of your own. They are the home screen. There is no app drawer behind it.

Nothing to scroll

Apps that are not tools stay closed. Open one and you are sent back home. Notifications from them are cleared before they buzz.

No browser, ever

A browser is never offered as a tool. One browser makes every other choice cosmetic, and that is most of what makes a dumb phone work.

How it works

Ten minutes of setup, then it stays out of your way.

Pick your tools

Tick the roles you want on the home screen and choose which installed app fills each one. Skip anything you do not use.

Choose your wait

One, three, six or twelve hours. This is what turning it off, or adding a tool later, will cost you. Choose a wait you would respect on a bad day.

Quiet the rest

Notifications from anything that is not a tool are cleared before they reach you. Calls, alarms and emergency alerts always come through.

Turn it on and live with it

You turn on app blocking yourself in Android's settings, after a plain explanation of what it does. Then the home screen is just your tools.

Changing your mind

Turning it off is allowed. It just takes a while.

A dumb phone works because getting the old phone back is a decision, not a reflex. Anchor Dumb Phone makes it a wait: a screen that counts down only while it stays open.

It counts down only while you watch it

Set the phone down and let it run. Open anything else and it pauses. Away for more than two minutes and the change is dropped; start it again when you are ready. Calls do not count against you.

Removing is instant, adding waits

Take a tool off the home screen any time. Adding one back, or turning the whole thing off, means sitting out the wait you chose.

During the trial, every wait is five minutes

Long enough to feel how it works, short enough that nobody is stuck before they have decided. The wait you chose starts with the purchase.

There is always a floor

Android Safe Mode turns the app off on any phone. Emergency calls, alarms and emergency alerts never wait.

Questions

The things people ask first.

What if I need an app I did not pick?

Add it as a tool from Settings. It joins the home screen after your wait. Up to three apps outside the standard set can be tools, so a banking app or a work app is fine. Pick those at setup if you can, because adding later waits.

Do links in text messages open?

No. A browser is never a tool, so a link has nowhere to go. That is on purpose, and the setup screen says so before you commit.

Can I still get calls and alarms?

Always. Phone calls including emergency calls, alarms, and government emergency alerts are never silenced and never wait.

What happens when the trial ends?

Blocking turns off on its own and the home screen offers the unlock. The trial is free and nothing is charged unless you buy it.

Is there a way out if something goes wrong?

Yes. The app's own off switch, behind the wait you chose. And Android Safe Mode turns the app off on any phone without waiting. The app never blocks uninstall entirely.

Why does it need the accessibility service?

It is how an Android app can notice which app is in front, which is what sending a non-tool app back home requires. The app uses it for that and for backing out of the settings pages that would turn blocking off before its wait is done. It does not read your screen content, record anything, or send data anywhere. You turn it on yourself, in Android's settings, after a plain explanation.

Does it work with Anchor?

They are separate apps. Anchor is the one where a partner answers your requests. Anchor Dumb Phone is solo: the wait is the only thing between you and the old phone. Pick whichever fits how you want to be held to it.
