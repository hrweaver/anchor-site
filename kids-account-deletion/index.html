Deleting your Anchor Kids account and data

Apps: Anchor Kids (com.anchorapp.kids) and Anchor Kids for Parents (com.anchorapp.kidsmanager) Developer: Anchor Digital LLC

There are two things you may want to delete: a single child's profile, or your whole account. The first you can do yourself in the app. The second we do for you on request.

Delete a child's profile

You can do this yourself at any time.

  1. Open Anchor Kids for Parents and sign in.
  2. Tap the child you want to remove.
  3. Scroll to Remove this profile.
  4. Type the child's name to confirm.

This permanently deletes that child's profile and everything stored under it: their approved contacts, call history, message records, contact requests, alerts, unlock requests and PIN attempt records. It cannot be undone.

Removing the profile does not wipe the child's phone. To return the phone to normal, uninstall Anchor Kids from it through Android Settings.

Delete your whole account

The app does not yet have a self-serve account deletion button. To delete your account, email support@anchorph.one from the address your account uses, with the subject "Delete my Anchor Kids account".

We delete the account and its data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign in
  • Your name and email address
  • Every child profile in your family, and everything under them: contacts, call history, message records, contact requests, alerts, location, device status and tamper records
  • Your family and its membership records
  • Any pairing codes or family invites you created

What is kept, and for how long

  • Crash reports are held for up to 90 days, then deleted. They are not linked to your name or email.
  • Backups and logs containing personal data are purged within 30 days of deletion.

What never left the phone in the first place

The text of your child's messages and their call log are read on their device to display them in the Anchor Kids app. They are never uploaded, so there is nothing on our side to delete. They go when the app is uninstalled from the phone.
